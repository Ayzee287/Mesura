Studio, 7-Zip, Spotify, Steam, Telegram, AyuGram, Visual Studio Code, Vencord). They belong to their owners, are used only to show which
app is meant, and imply no endorsement. They were taken from each app's own executable or the vendor's official site or
repository. If an owner objects to one, it is removed and the app shows its initial instead. Mesura is not affiliated
with any of them, with Microsoft, or with NVIDIA.

## Tools Mesura starts but does not ship

Mesura downloads or runs these only when you ask, through winget or from their own pages; none is part of the release
files: Windows Package Manager (winget), NVCleanstall (TechPowerUp), NVIDIA Profile Inspector (Orbmu2k) and the
installers of the apps in the Apps catalog. Each is under its own terms.

comes from. They are cited, not copied; the sentences in the catalog are Mesura's own.
