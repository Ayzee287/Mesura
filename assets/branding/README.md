# Mesura logo

An M measured with a dimension line: the M is the product, the mint line under it with its two end caps is the
measurement. Near-black tile, off-white M (`#EDF0F7`), mint line (`#62D6B8`, the app's accent), a faint inner edge so the
tile holds on a dark surface and the dark tile holds on a light one.

- `mesura-logo.svg` is the master. `mesura-logo-small.svg` is the variant used up to 32 px (heavier strokes, no end
  caps), because the master's fine parts blur at that size.
- `mesura-<n>.png` are renders of those at 16 to 512 px; the Windows icon (`src/Mesura.App/Assets/mesura.ico`) holds
  16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64, 128 and 256 px frames.
- Everything is generated by `tools/make-branding.ps1` from the numbers in that script. Nothing was traced, no stock
  image, font or third-party mark was used. It is Mesura's own work under the repository's MIT licence.
